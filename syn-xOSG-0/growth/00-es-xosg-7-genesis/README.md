# 00 — ES-xOSG-7 Genesis

## Role

ES-xOSG-7 is the genesis condition for this developmental path.

```text
ES-xOSG-7 = GENESIS
```

This stage is intentionally not called Seed.

A genesis system may be larger, more exploratory, less reduced, and more internally redundant than the final seed. Its purpose is to provide enough structure and variation for useful invariants to be discovered.

## What this stage contributes

- a broad experimental gestalt;
- initial orchestration concepts;
- enough complexity to expose contradictions and hidden assumptions;
- the source material from which later control structures can be extracted.

## Rule

Do not retroactively treat later invariants as though they were already present here unless the historical evidence supports that claim.
