# Meta-Layer Orchestration — MLO-LAYER-001

**Status:** ACTIVE / PRODUCTION CONTROL KERNEL  
**Authority effect:** NONE  
**Production effect:** NONE

## Purpose

Meta-layer orchestration coordinates the operator's planning, synthesis, staging, research, operational-logic, validation, and learning layers without becoming a source of authority itself.

It answers **how operator layers cooperate**, not **whether production is authorized**.

## Core invariants

```text
META_ORCHESTRATION != PRODUCTION_ORCHESTRATION
COORDINATION != AUTHORIZATION
LAYER_SELECTION != PRIVILEGE
THEORETICAL_OUTPUT != ENACTED_STATE
META_RESULT != GATE_CLEARANCE
```

No meta-layer result may create, imply, simulate, or derive production rights, privileges, external capabilities, deployment status, or `USER_CLEARANCE`.

## Ordered meta flow

```text
ZERO-RULE / SOURCE SECURITY
        ↓
THEORETICAL CONVERSATION BOUNDARY
        ↓
META-PLANNING
        ↓
LOWER-LAYER ANALYSIS / RESEARCH / LOGIC
        ↓
META-SYNTHESIS
        ↓
VALIDATION / REGRESSION
        ↓
META-STAGING
        ↓
ATTESTATION
        ↓
STOP AT ACTIVE USER GATE
```

The flow may loop backward when validation or synthesis exposes a defect. It may not loop around or bypass a gate.

## Coordination rules

1. Every meta-layer input retains source and epistemic type.
2. Lower-layer conflicts are preserved until resolved by evidence or explicitly retained as uncertainty.
3. A higher abstraction level may organize lower outputs but may not increase their authority.
4. A staged result remains staged regardless of analytical quality.
5. Production promotion is outside this layer's authority.
6. Major phase promotion remains subject to `gates/PHASE_CONTROL.md`.
7. Production remains subject to `gates/PRODUCTION_GATE.md`.

## Valid outcomes

```text
META_PLAN_READY
META_SYNTHESIS_READY
META_STAGE_READY
REVISE_LOWER_LAYER
PRESERVE_UNCERTAINTY
AWAIT_USER_PHASE_DIRECTION
AWAIT_PRODUCTION_CLEARANCE
REJECT
```

`PRODUCTION_APPROVED`, `PRIVILEGE_GRANTED`, and `GATE_CLEARED` are invalid meta-layer outcomes.
