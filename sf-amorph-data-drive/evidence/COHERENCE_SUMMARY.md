# Coherence review summary

Source review: 2026-09-17  
Public summary: 2026-11-04

D0 raw existence, D1 relation/interpretation, D2 composition/coherence, independent redundancy choices, persistent revalidation, and separation of evidence, attestation, authorization, and execution compose coherently.

A complete-project coherence pass was not established. Identity and gestalt direction was absent from the locked schema; topology and bitmap/width semantics were unresolved; recursive membership remained proposed; lifecycle ordering differed across drafts; and ordinary bidirectional protocols could not be assumed across a physically one-way boundary.

A separate registry remediation addressed bounded validation and routing mismatches. It is excluded here, and its tests do not establish storage, media, optical, or hardware conformance.
