Current environment wall
------------------------

- libOpenCL.so.1 is present but OpenCL headers are absent.
- Torch is CPU-only; CUDA/MPS/XPU are unavailable.
- Therefore an independent compiled MPFR transcendental oracle and real GPU/open-compute runtime certification cannot be completed honestly on this host.
