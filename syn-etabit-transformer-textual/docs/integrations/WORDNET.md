# Princeton WordNet 3.1 integration barrier

The WordNet payload is intentionally absent from this repository.

## Recorded source identity

- Publisher: Princeton University
- Edition: WordNet 3.1 database-only archive
- Official URL: `https://wordnetcode.princeton.edu/wn3.1.dict.tar.gz`
- Recorded bytes: `16,358,468`
- Recorded SHA-256: `3f7d8be8ef6ecc7167d39b10d66954ec734280b5bdcd57f7d9eafe429d11c22a`
- Embedded release notice: WordNet 3.1, copyright 2011 Princeton University

The recorded hash identifies previously retrieved bytes. It is not a publisher signature and does not independently prove authenticity or trusted time.

## Retrieval boundary

If a later approved scope requires the archive:

1. retrieve it from the authoritative publisher URL;
2. retain the upstream notice and acquisition receipt;
3. verify the complete gzip read and archive structure;
4. compare the byte length and SHA-256 with the recorded identity;
5. place the payload in an integration cache outside tracked source;
6. do not execute bundled programs;
7. do not bulk-extract, parse or load records without a separately approved intake; and
8. preserve raw source fields and locators before decoding or normalization.

## Semantic boundary

WordNet supplies candidate spellings, source relations and source metadata. It does not define the native symbol, glyph, component, root, word, sentence or paragraph hierarchy. Equal spellings do not imply equal senses. Morphy rules and exception pairs do not provide complete native construction.

Future Etabit transmigration must keep source, decoded, constructed, inferred and synthesized maps separate, linked by traceable derivations. Grammar, syntax and Government-and-Binding projections remain relative and replaceable.

The earlier bounded vocabulary pilot does not authorize full-corpus absorption, durable loading, GPU conversion or agentic activation.
