# Candidate generic relation catalog

All 17 entries in this catalog are **PROPOSED_NOT_ADOPTED**. They are project-level address and composition vocabulary, not recovered WordNet facts and not Government-and-Binding relations.

The candidates cover four base concerns:

- objects and references: `references`, `member_of`, `contains`;
- orders and structures: `precedes`, `ordered_by`, `structured_by`, `classified_in`;
- processes and provenance: `produced_by`, `derived_from`, `annotated_by`;
- relative contexts and evidence: `selected_in_context`, `replaces_in_context`, `supported_by`, `contradicted_by`, `unresolved_against`.

`related_to` is deliberately identified as an underspecified placeholder. `alternative_to` is weaker than antonymy. `derived_from` is a provenance edge and must not silently replace WordNet's derivationally-related relation. A future adoption decision may specialize, rename or reject any candidate while retaining this study as evidence.

Exact records are in `generic_relation_catalog.json`.
