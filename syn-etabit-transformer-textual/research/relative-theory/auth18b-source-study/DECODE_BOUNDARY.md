# AUTH-18B decode boundary

## What may be decoded now

- the already retained AUTH-18A WordNet record fields;
- pointer symbols and source/target word-position tuples under Princeton's format documentation;
- WordNet-defined adjective markers and structural labels;
- explicit catalog IDs, statuses, source references and crosswalk links;
- project-level candidate generic edges, always marked proposed rather than adopted.

## What may not be decoded now

- Government-and-Binding relations, constraints, domains or transformations;
- roots, prefixes, suffixes or morphological analyses inferred from spelling;
- syntactic function inferred from WordNet pointers or gloss prose;
- equivalence between a generic project relation and a source-defined relation;
- any corpus-wide WordNet map, database or live Etabit representation;
- any replacement of original source identity with derived enrichment.

## Required future evidence

Formal theory decoding requires an accessible designated governing edition, reviewed locators for every adopted rule, conflict treatment across editions or sources, a schema proposal and a newly approved bounded implementation/validation scope. The current inspector is intentionally unable to execute instructions or transformation rules from its data.
